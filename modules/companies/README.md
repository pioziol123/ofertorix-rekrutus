# Companies

Companies gathers information about companies and records their roles in recruitments. [Module map](../../docs/architecture/moduly.md)

## Public operations

Routes, the worker and other modules import operations from `index.ts`. Views run in the browser, so they have a separate entry `view.ts`, which does not reach into server code.

| Operation                                                        | Entry      | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Company creation (`createCompany`)                               | `index.ts` | Takes a command identifier, the name, kind, website and description. The same command gives the same answer; a reused identifier with other content ends with `CompanyConflict`.                                                                       |
| Link change (`changeCompanyLink`)                                | `index.ts` | Links a company to an ad in a role, or removes the link, with the evidence and the expected current company; records the change with the ad version read. An unknown company or ad ends with `CompanyNotFound`, a changed link with `CompanyConflict`. |
| Company reads (`listCompanies`, `readCompany`, `offerCompanies`) | `index.ts` | The list with search, sort and pages of 25; a company with its offers, technologies and link history; the companies of an offer and suggestions by the offer's company name.                                                                           |
| Research (`requestResearch`, `readCompanyResearch`)              | `index.ts` | Described in Research through AI below.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Labels for views                                                 | `view.ts`  | Company kinds and roles, research topics, kinds and scopes, and whether a report has sourced findings.                                                                                                                                                 |

## Data

| Data                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Research            | One request for basic research of a company: the target (name, kind, website, description) fixed when it was requested, and its outcome. Its state holds only Companies' meaning, `requested`. ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-0mq.22/ASM-1)                                             |
| Link to the AI work | A research keeps the identifier of the agent assignment placed for it; its state is read from Companies' own data. Research made before oferty-pracy-sq2.10 has none, and its link to the earlier AI records was dropped at the deployment of assignments (oferty-pracy-sq2.11). |
| Outcome             | The published report, or the owner's message when the assignment failed or Companies did not accept the report. A report is never removed: every published version stays available.                                                                                              |
| Research commands   | Each command the owner sent, with the answer it got, so the same command gives the same answer and creates nothing more.                                                                                                                                                         |
| Read-only history   | The attempt link and the price-list snapshot (`configuration`) of research made before `oferty-pracy-0mq.22`. No code reads them; a new research writes `'{}'` in the configuration, as AI keeps the settings of its work. ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-0mq.22/ASM-1)                 |

## Research through AI

[research-ai.ts](infrastructure/adapters/research-ai.ts) translates a research into one agent assignment with web search under the settings name `research` (epic sq2, T9): the instruction for the search, the instruction for writing the report from the gathered evidence units, the response format (derived from the report draft contract), the target as the input and the company's name as the label.

- **A request** ([request-research.ts](request-research.ts)) reuses the company's research still in progress, and its latest published report unless the owner asks for a refresh. A research is in progress until Companies saved its outcome, read from Companies' own data alone, also for research made before assignments; one still in progress at the deployment of assignments is ended by the migration step, below. ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.10/ASM-1) Requests of one company are decided one at a time under a transaction lock, so a company has at most one research in progress. Otherwise the research is saved with the assignment placed for it in one transaction. Research that cannot be requested now (missing settings or secret, asked of the AI module without calling a provider) is refused before anything is saved.
- **The receiver** of assignments ([receive-research.ts](receive-research.ts)) is registered in the shared registration of receivers and runs in the AI module's transaction that saves the assignment's final status. On a result it validates the report draft against the materials the agent gathered (every topic covered, each fact and opinion quoting an evidence unit exactly, only safe sources, at least one finding backed by a source) and publishes it, or ends the research as failed with the owner's message; either way the assignment succeeds. On a failure it ends the research with the message of a failed assignment, which names no cause and points to the AI assignments panel. ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.10/ASM-3) It changes only a research whose outcome is not saved yet, so the same end delivered again changes nothing. Once a research has ended, a new one can be requested.
- **What the owner sees** of research with an assignment: "Trwa zbieranie źródeł i przygotowywanie raportu" until it ends, then the report, or "Nie udało się przygotować raportu" with its message ([research-view.ts](domain/research-view.ts)). No amount is shown: the cost is in the AI assignments panel, and the view no longer shows the most one research reserves. ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.10/ASM-2)

**Research made before the deployment of assignments** (task oferty-pracy-sq2.11) shows only what Companies stored itself: a saved report stays visible, with no cost, and research that ended without a report is not shown. Research still in progress at the deployment is ended by the migration step through `failResearchOfOldWay` ([fail-research-of-old-way.ts](fail-research-of-old-way.ts)) with the message of a failed assignment, so it shows as "Nie udało się przygotować raportu" and new research can be requested. That message is how the view tells it apart from research that ended before the deployment, which never got it (ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.11/ASM-1), [research-view.ts](domain/research-view.ts)).
