# AI

The `modules/ai/` module carries out the AI work the other modules ask for, as **assignments** (zlecenia AI): a requesting module places an assignment in its own save and learns of its one end, a result or a failure, through its receiver. The AI module chooses the executor, carries out the assignment's operations one after another, retries them, keeps the work within the monthly budget, ends every assignment within its time limit and keeps the exchange with the provider. This description covers the first version of assignments (epic `oferty-pracy-sq2`, [requirements](../../docs/specs/sq2-zlecenia-ai/requirements.md), [solution design](../../docs/specs/sq2-zlecenia-ai/solution-design.md)); the terms follow the AI section of the [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md#ai-module). [Module map](../../docs/architecture/moduly.md)

## Division of responsibility

| Part                      | Responsibility                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Requesting module         | Its own matter (a proposal preparation, an assessment, a research), the assignment identifier it keeps with it, its receiver, the check of a result by its own rules, and what its views show. It guards its matter against a second request in progress itself.    |
| Assignment handling       | `assignments/`: assignments and operations with their statuses and states, the order of work, the budget, retries, the time limit and the delivery of the end. The only code that changes an assignment's status or an operation's state.                           |
| Executor                  | `executors/`: one submodule per executor. Its declaration (services, parameters, price lists, maximum run times), carrying out one operation, the translation of its provider's events into the four outcomes, and its own state between the steps of an operation. |
| Settings of assignments   | `assignments/assignment-settings.ts`: the fixed table from a settings name to the executor, its parameters and the requesting module's display name.                                                                                                                |
| AI module plugin          | [plugins/ai.ts](../../plugins/ai.ts): registers the job of each executor's queue with its handler and failure handler; only the worker and its pool processes run it.                                                                                               |
| Registration of receivers | [receivers/index.ts](../../receivers/index.ts): each requesting module's receiver under its settings name; the web application, the periodic process, the worker and its pool processes run it at start.                                                            |
| Periodic process          | [periodic/steps.ts](../../periodic/steps.ts): calls the operations waiting for the budget, the time limit and the daily ad syncs, in this order.                                                                                                                    |

An assignment is in progress, needs attention, succeeded or failed; succeeded and failed are final. An operation is pending (waiting for the budget or for its executor), running, succeeded, failed or uncertain. A failed operation carries a cause from one closed list and whether it may be retried. A failed assignment records one reason for the owner: `rejected`, `time_limit` or `delivery_failed`. The requesting module sees only the status and never a cost of its assignments, a cause or a reason; the potential cost of a price list is readable for display (ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.11/ASM-2)).

## Public operations

Requesting modules and entry points import these from [index.ts](index.ts). The panel's pages import the shapes of its reads and their texts (statuses, operation states, reasons, types and causes) from [view.ts](view.ts), which reaches no server code:

| Operation                          | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `placeAssignment`                  | In the caller's transaction, places an assignment: the settings name, the type (`single` or `agent`), a label for the owner, the instruction, the input, optionally a response format, and for an agent the tools from `agentTools` and the result instruction. The executor and its parameters found under the settings name are frozen with the content. The assignment starts in progress with one pending operation waiting for its executor, whose job is sent to the executor's queue; its time limit is 7 days from placing. A refusal throws `AssignmentError` with a code from its closed list before anything is created. With `refuseOversized` a request larger than the executor's price list covers is refused. Each call places a new assignment: the AI module keeps no identifier of the requesting module's matter. |
| `readAssignmentStatuses`           | Given identifiers, the status of each known assignment (`assignmentStatuses`: in progress, needs attention, succeeded, failed) and the identifiers not found. Nothing else: no operations, costs, causes or reasons.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `readAssignmentExchange`           | Given an assignment identifier, the exchange kept with each of its operations, from the first: every request as sent and every raw response as received, in the order the executor wrote them; null for an unknown identifier. It holds the requesting module's input and the provider's full output, so its one consumer is Matching's view of an assessment's provider exchange.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `canPlaceAssignments`              | Whether assignments of a type can be placed under a settings name now: the settings are known and complete, the executor serves the type with those parameters and has its secret or address. No provider is called.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `readPotentialCost`                | The potential cost of one operation of an assignment of a type under a settings name now, the most it holds of the month's budget when it starts; null when such assignments cannot be placed. No provider is called. Matching shows it as the most one assessment reserves (ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.11/ASM-2), ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.9/ASM-3)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `readMonthlyBudgets`               | Every month with a budget, newest first: its limit, the amount settled, the amount held and their sum, the month's charge (see Budget below). | `readAssignmentList`               | One page of the AI assignments panel's list (T11-R02): every assignment, those needing attention first, then the others, each group from the newest placed, 50 at a time. A row gives the label, the type, the requesting module's display name from the settings (the settings name itself when the settings no longer hold it), the executor, the status, the reason of a failed one, the time left to the time limit for one not yet final and the budget charge. The page's `nextCursor` gives the next page; a cursor the read did not write gives an empty page.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

No request, raw response or result. Null for an unknown identifier.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `countAssignmentsNeedingAttention` | The number of assignments needing attention, shown in the navigation of every page when above zero (T11-R04).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

The settings of requesting modules for assignments are a fixed table in `assignments/assignment-settings.ts`: a settings name gives an executor, its parameters and the requesting module's display name. It holds the Profile's entry, `profile`, Matching's, `matching`, and Companies', `research`, all with the paid executor. Each executor declares, in `executors/`, its name, the job of its queue, and per assignment type the parameter sets it offers with their price lists, the agent tools it offers and its maximum run time; the paid executor (`openai`) and the local executor (`ollama`) read them from the AI configuration.

Every executor carries out one operation through the same contract (`executors/declaration.ts`): it gets the assignment's frozen content, the executor parameters, its own saved state and two writers, one for the exchange and one for its state, each conditional on the operation still running. It returns one of four outcomes from `executors/outcome.ts`: a result, an ordinary failure, a non-retryable failure or uncertain, each failure with a cause from the one closed list of causes, and the usage it knows. Which outcome an event of its provider means is decided in the executor alone, by the mapping of the [solution design](../../docs/specs/sq2-zlecenia-ai/solution-design.md#t2); no executor repeats a provider call inside an operation. The executor ends its own work when its maximum run time passes (2 minutes for a paid single answer, 15 for a paid agent session, 30 for a local single answer, all below the 60 minutes of the queues) and reports an ordinary failure. A result is the content the provider returned, parsed as JSON when the assignment gave a response format; the AI module does not compare it with the format. The exact request and every raw response are kept in `ai_operation_exchange`, written before a request is sent and before a response is read. Assignment handling computes the actual cost from the usage and the price list saved when the operation started (`assignments/operation-cost.ts`). The executors send the same requests to OpenAI and Ollama as the adapters of the old way did; their tests pin those requests.

The AI module plugin registers `runOperation` (`assignments/run-operation.ts`) as the handler of every executor's queue (`executorJobs`), and `endFailedOperation` as its failure handler. Each queue has exclusivity on a constant key, so an executor carries out one operation at a time in the whole system, in the order its jobs were sent, while different executors work side by side. The handler has three steps:

1. **Start**, one transaction with the assignment's and the operation's rows locked (`assignments/start-operation.ts`). An operation waiting for its executor of an assignment in progress gets its potential cost from the price list its executor declares now: the cost of the price list's caps, as the old way reserved an attempt. Free work (no price list) runs with no budget month and charges nothing. Otherwise the current month's row is locked, created with the default limit when missing, and the operation runs with its potential cost, its month in Polish time and the price list used when the room covers it; else it waits for the budget and its job ends, so the executor takes its next operation. Room is the month's limit less its carried amounts and the charges of the month's operations. A pending operation of an assignment no longer in progress ends as failed with the cause `not_started`. An operation already running was left so by an attempt that ended or was lost (the queue runs one attempt of a job at a time); it is interrupted, described below. An operation whose executor no longer offers its frozen parameters does not start: its attempt fails (ASSUMPTION(oferty-pracy-sq2.4/ASM-1)).
2. **Carry out**, outside any transaction: the executor is called once.
3. **End**, one transaction (`assignments/end-operation.ts`): a write conditional on the operation still running saves the state, the result or the cause with whether it may be retried, the usage and the actual cost from the saved price list, or unknown. Then `assignments/after-operation-end.ts` decides what follows for the assignment, described below; after a result the handler delivers it, described below.

**What follows the end of an operation** is decided in one place (`assignments/after-operation-end.ts`), in the transaction that ended the operation, with the assignment's row locked; every path that ends an operation uses it. An assignment that is no longer in progress is left as it is. An ordinary failure of an automatic operation, while the assignment has fewer automatic operations than its limit (3, stored when it was placed), adds the next operation in the same save: pending, waiting for its executor, with its job sent to the end of the executor's queue, carrying out the same frozen content. Any other failure, the third automatic one, a non-retryable one or one of an operation the owner added, and every uncertain end move the assignment to needs attention, a change conditional on it still being in progress; nothing of it runs or waits then. An ended operation no longer holds its executor, which takes its next job. A failed or uncertain end once the assignment's deadline has passed gets no further operation: the assignment needs attention, and the time limit fails it at its next pass.

**Receivers.** A requesting module learns of every end of its assignments, a result or a failure, only through its receiver (`AssignmentReceiver`, `assignments/assignment-receiver.ts`), never from the status read. It has two entries: a result, with the assignment identifier, the result and, for an agent, the materials of its session; and a failure, with the assignment identifier alone. Neither returns a value, and neither gets a cost, a cause or a reason. Each call runs inside the AI module's transaction that saves the assignment's final status, so the receiver's own saves and that status are committed together or not at all. Every receiver follows three rules: a call repeated with the same end changes nothing more; it uses only what the call gives it and asks the AI module nothing; its module does not assume that it learns of an end at the moment the end happened. A requesting module registers its receiver with `registerAssignmentReceiver` under its settings name in the shared registration `receivers/index.ts`, outside the modules, which the web application, the periodic process, the worker and its pool processes each run at start; a second registration of a name throws. It holds the Profile's, Matching's and Companies' receivers.

**Delivery of a result** (`assignments/deliver-result.ts`). Right after the end that saved a result, and when a later attempt of the job finds its operation succeeded with the assignment in progress, the handler opens a delivery transaction in the same process; no job is sent for it. With the assignment's row locked and the assignment in progress, it calls the receiver's result entry and sets the assignment succeeded. When the receiver raises an error, or no receiver is registered under the settings name, that transaction rolls back and a second one, conditional on the assignment still being in progress, fails it with the reason `delivery_failed` (result not taken) and calls the receiver's failure entry when there is one; the operation's cost stays as it was settled. Any other error, the receiver's error on the failure included, fails the attempt, and the next attempt repeats the delivery from its beginning without calling the executor. After the last attempt the failure handler leaves a succeeded operation as it is, and so does the time limit: the assignment stays in progress.

**Time limit** (`failAssignmentsPastTimeLimit`, `assignments/fail-assignments-past-time-limit.ts`), a step of the periodic process after the operations waiting for the budget. Every assignment in progress or needing attention whose deadline, 7 days after placing, has passed fails with the reason `time_limit`, unless it has a running operation, which may still end with a result, or is in progress with a succeeded operation whose delivery is still to be made. Each assignment ends in its own transaction, conditional on the rule still holding with its row locked: its pending operations end as failed with the cause `not_started`, charging nothing, and its receiver's failure entry is called. An error rolls that transaction back and is logged with the assignment's identifier; the other assignments are still handled, and the next pass tries again, so an assignment whose receiver always fails stays unfinished.

**The last attempt failed.** The worker calls `endFailedOperation` (`assignments/end-failed-operation.ts`) after a job of an operation failed on its last attempt, also when the worker died in it. A pending operation, whose work failed before it started, ends as an ordinary failure with the cause `unknown` and nothing charged, and what follows the end is decided as above. An operation found running is interrupted, described below; any other state is left as it is. The attempts of a job never repeat the executor's call, and nothing in assignment handling or in an executor repeats a provider call after a transient error: the next operation is the only retry.

**Interrupted operations.** An operation found running by a later attempt of its job, or by the failure handler, had its work stop without an outcome: its process was ended for the queue's time limit, or lost with the worker or a deployment. It ends `uncertain` with the cause `unknown` (`assignments/end-interrupted-operation.ts`): its actual cost stays unknown and its potential cost stays charged to its month, and what follows the end moves its assignment to needs attention. The executor is not called again, also when nothing had been sent, and the AI module does not work out why the work stopped; no executor declares that an operation may be repeated. A lost attempt is found by the queue within about 30–90 s and the next attempt, or the failure handler after the last one, follows at once, well within five minutes. A process that returns after its operation was ended saves nothing: its end and its writers are conditional on the operation still running, and what it received is in the exchange already.

The periodic process calls `sendOperationsWaitingForBudget` in each pass: it reads the current month's room once and walks the operations waiting for the budget of assignments in progress, oldest first; each whose potential cost at today's prices fits what remains waits for its executor again, with its job sent to the end of the executor's queue. Nothing is charged there; the start checks the budget again.

A month's charge is its two carried amounts plus the sum, over its operations, of the actual cost when known and the potential cost otherwise; nothing else stores a sum. `readMonthlyBudgets` gives every month with a budget, newest first, with its limit, the amount settled (the carried settled amount and the known actual costs) and the amount held (the carried held amount and the potential costs of running operations and of those with an unknown cost). `assignments/read-assignment-cost.ts` computes an assignment's sum of potential costs, sum of known actual costs, budget charge and number of operations with an unknown cost, for the panel. The limit a new month gets is kept in the one-row table `ai_budget_default_limit`, 100 PLN, the amount every month has started with so far.

## Earlier records and the deployment

Before assignments, the AI module ran an older contract of executions, with provider batches, a check of a result against its response format and its own states and causes given to the requesting modules. Task `oferty-pracy-sq2.11` removed it from the code. Its records stay in the database as an archive that no code reads: the tables `archive_ai_executions`, `archive_ai_attempts`, `archive_ai_execution_artifacts` and `archive_matching_batch_artifacts`. The executions lost the four unused columns of the former Profile path; Matching's earlier batch and group tables are untouched. The queues `ai-execution-paid` and `ai-execution-local` were deleted with their jobs.

The deployment carried the budget over. Each month with a budget kept its limit and got two carried amounts in `ai_budget_months`, computed from the old settlement records: the settled costs as `carried_settled`, and the reserved amounts of records whose cost is unknown, whose work had started or whose result waited to be taken as `carried_held`. Work queued or waiting for the budget had sent nothing, so its reservation was released. The migration step then has each requesting module mark as failed its matters still waiting for work of the old way ([scripts/end-old-ai-work.ts](../../scripts/end-old-ai-work.ts)), so the owner requests them again; each module decides from its own data alone. The histories of Matching and Companies show of earlier work only what they stored themselves.

## Adding a requesting module or an executor

A module that needs AI work adds a settings name and a receiver; a new provider or runtime adds an executor. Neither changes the modules that already place assignments.

**A new settings name.** Add the entry with its executor, parameters and display name to [assignment-settings.ts](assignments/assignment-settings.ts); the parameters must be an offer the executor declares. In the module's integration layer, translate the request into an assignment's content and place it with `placeAssignment` in the module's own save, keeping the identifier with its matter. Implement an `AssignmentReceiver` that follows the three rules above and register it under the settings name in [receivers/index.ts](../../receivers/index.ts). The Profile's [proposal-ai.ts](../profile/infrastructure/adapters/proposal-ai.ts) is the shortest example.

**A new executor.** Implement the executor contract of [declaration.ts](executors/declaration.ts): a name, the job of its own queue (created by its own queue migration, with exclusivity on a constant key), the services it offers per assignment type with their parameters, price lists and maximum run time, whether it is configured, and carrying out one operation that returns one of the outcomes of [outcome.ts](executors/outcome.ts). Register it in [registry.ts](executors/registry.ts) and give its queue an entry in [queues.json](../../queues.json). Its unit tests cover the mapping of each event of its provider to an outcome, as those of the two executors do.

## Simplifications of the first version

- **One operation at a time per executor.** A long company research, or many assessments, make other paid work wait; more at a time is put off.
- **Every interrupted operation needs the owner**, also when nothing had been sent: no executor declares that an operation may be repeated.
- **A result the receiver does not take is lost** with its cost: the assignment fails with `delivery_failed`, and nothing delivers it again.
- **A cost the provider did not report** stays charged to its month at the potential cost; settling it is task `oferty-pracy-qld`.
- **The settings are fixed in code**, and so is the choice of executor; how a requesting module's preferences choose one belongs to epic `oferty-pracy-334`.
- **The panel only reads so far.** The page "Zlecenia AI" lists assignments and shows their details (task `oferty-pracy-sq2.12`); Retry, Reject and changing the limits come with tasks `oferty-pracy-sq2.13` and `sq2.14`, and until then the owner acts on an assignment needing attention in the database.

Together these make the module a single path for AI work: requesting modules know only an assignment identifier, its status and their receiver, and every rule of retries, money and time lives in assignment handling.
